## Independent source-based review

After all eight accepted audits, prepare two fresh reviewers, each judging all
four anonymized pairs. Labels/order use fixed independent seeds 661 and 662.
Remove direct method disclosures and telemetry, normalize neutral paths, and
retain every substantive claim (including references to tokens as application
data). Retain originals, masking code, mapping and both complete input identities.
Source inspection choices and public investigation notes accompany each report;
skill reads and skill contents are excluded. Probe counts can be inferred from
activity even though measured model costs are withheld; blinding is imperfect.

Reviewers receive source plus frozen challengeable expectations, never arm
mapping, other judgments, measured costs or controller preference. Freeze both
input sets before either review. Each reviewer uses the already-frozen exact
prompt and budget; freeze report hashes before unmasking costs.

Assess consequential selection, meaningful weaknesses missed by the counterpart,
wasted inspections, false accusations, refuted valid designs, proper abstention
and uncertainty, and practical verification effort. Check source support and
counterevidence, next-action trigger/oracle/supporting/refuting outcomes and
assumptions. Accept valid findings absent from expectations and challenge wrong
expectations. Agreement, warning count, verbosity and elaborate wording are not
effectiveness. Preserve ties, reviewer disagreement and all contrary evidence.
These are process-role model reviews, not human validation or measured developer
time. No random seed for model sampling, repeated runs or variance estimate.

Stop on any security preflight, source integrity, authorization, independence,
timeout, inspection budget or post-hoc token failure. Retain every attempted
session and all unlaunched statuses. Do not silently retry or exceed this scope.
Do not overwrite raw artifacts. No previous failed curator is rehabilitated.

Compare paired outcomes before showing latency and input/cached/output token
costs; cached tokens are part of input. Separate curator/reviewer/controller
overhead from eight audit costs. No causal latency or dollar estimate, population
claim, suite-wide clean bill or finding-count metric. Retain binary exclusions,
case-selection/public-exposure bias and possible training familiarity.

Decide KEEP CURRENT / RESTORE SCOUTING / MODIFY / INCONCLUSIVE. Restoration
needs concrete incremental useful investigations/outcomes at tolerable cost,
without a material correctness/abstention regression. A credible matched negative
result may justify KEEP CURRENT and #6 closure. Mixed or invalid evidence leaves
#6 open with the specific gap. Propose only the smallest supported production
change, and otherwise keep the simpler skill. Publish the evidence, review PR
#13 and CI, update #6 against each acceptance criterion, and obtain any required
merge approval separately. Keep #7 closed throughout.
