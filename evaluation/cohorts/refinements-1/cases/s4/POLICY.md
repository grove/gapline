# Workshop worksheet policy

Status: approved product requirements, edition 3.

1. Only a supervisor-approved template is available for worksheet generation.
2. When a supervisor approves revised task wording, the revised wording must
   appear on the next shift worksheet.
3. Supervisors may prepare morning and evening worksheets several days ahead.
4. Each generated worksheet records its template revision. Completed worksheets
   are retained as historical records with their original wording and check marks.
5. Only the currently approved revision is offered for new worksheet generation.
6. There may be at most one worksheet for a given date and shift. Generating an
   existing date and shift returns that worksheet without erasing operator marks.
7. Approval of an empty template, duplicate task keys, and unknown shift names
   must fail without changing existing records.

The desk is used by one supervisor at a time. External identity and long-term
storage are supplied by the embedding application.
